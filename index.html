About me

I’m a freelancer who works across customer support, QA, SEO, recruiting and 3D art, and I’m open to consulting projects. I like work with clear standards and real people on the other end.

I trained as a 3D digital artist and earned a bachelor’s degree at the Copenhagen Academy of Digital Arts. That gave me a habit of precision and technical detail, which I now apply to operations, QA and automation. In 2022 I interned at HATO Medical Technologies, modeling, texturing and animating an intelligent 3D heart for a medical tablet app, and I still take on selected 3D design and asset work.

Since then I’ve worked in customer service, responsible gaming and anti-money laundering at RoyalCasino, where I also moved into SEO and QA, followed by content moderation and fintech support. Along the way I’ve trained new colleagues and worked on the SOPs and training materials they learn from.
